# Initial Broad Discovery: 热带鱼家庭养殖的水质如何调节 (Tropical Fish Water Quality)

## Core Parameters (物理、化学、生物指标)
- **Temperature (水温):** 24°C - 28°C. Fluctuations over 2°C cause stress/disease.
- **pH (酸碱度):** 6.5 - 7.5. Stability is more important than absolute value. Lowered by driftwood, raised by coral sand.
- **Ammonia (NH₃/NH₄⁺) & Nitrite (NO₂⁻):** Must be exactly 0 mg/L. Highly toxic.
- **Nitrate (NO₃⁻):** < 40 mg/L. End product of the nitrogen cycle, less toxic.
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO):** 5 - 8 mg/L. Below 3mg/L causes "floating head" (asphyxiation).
- **Hardness (GH/KH):** Impacts osmotic pressure and pH buffering capacity.

## Regulation Methods (调节方法)
- **Biological Filtration (生物过滤):** The core mechanism. Cultivating nitrifying bacteria to convert ammonia to nitrate.
- **Water Changes (科学换水):** 1/5 - 1/4 weekly. Tap water must be dechlorinated (aged 2-3 days). Temperature difference ≤ ±1°C.
- **Physical/Chemical Regulation:** Heating rods, cooling fans. Using buffering agents or natural items (almond leaves vs coral sand) for pH.
- **Auxiliary Methods:** Aquatic plants (absorb nitrates), controlled feeding (prevents rotting organic matter), active carbon.
