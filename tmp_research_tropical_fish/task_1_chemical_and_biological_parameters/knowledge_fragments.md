## Toxicity Metrics: Un-ionized Ammonia (NH3)
The 96-hour LC50 (Lethal Concentration for 50% of the population over 96 hours) for un-ionized ammonia ($NH_3$) in tropical fish generally ranges from **0.1 mg/L to 3.0 mg/L**. Toxicity is heavily dependent on species, life stage, and water chemistry.
- **Sensitive Tropical Species (e.g., Cardinal Tetras):** LC50 is extremely low, around **0.36 mg/L**. Other sensitive species like Rinuak show LC50 around 0.27 mg/L.
- **Warmwater / Tropical Fish (e.g., Guppies):** LC50 is around **1.17 mg/L** (males are more sensitive than females).
- **Hardy Aquaculture Species (e.g., Tilapia):** LC50 ranges from **1.0 mg/L** for larvae up to **7.4 mg/L** for fingerlings.

*Thresholds:*
- **Safe Level:** **< 0.02 mg/L** ($NH_3$). Most species show no long-term adverse effects.
- **Chronic Stress Level:** **0.05 – 0.15 mg/L** ($NH_3$). Exposure causes gill damage, reduced growth, and suppressed immune systems.
- **Acute Danger:** **> 0.20 mg/L** ($NH_3$). Immediate action is required.
- **Lethal:** **> 2.0 mg/L** ($NH_3$). Most fish begin to die rapidly.

## pH, Temperature, and Un-ionized Ammonia Relationship
The toxicity of Total Ammonia Nitrogen (TAN) shifts dramatically with pH and temperature. As pH or temperature rises, the proportion of toxic un-ionized ammonia ($NH_3$) increases. Because pH is logarithmic, a 1-unit increase in pH (e.g., from 7.0 to 8.0) increases the toxic ammonia concentration by roughly 10 times.

*Percentage of Un-ionized Ammonia (NH3) from TAN at 25°C (77°F):*
- At pH 7.0: 0.57%
- At pH 7.2: 0.90%
- At pH 7.4: 1.42%
- At pH 7.6: 2.23%
- At pH 7.8: 3.49%
- At pH 8.0: 5.41%
- At pH 8.2: 8.32%
- At pH 8.4: 12.6%
- At pH 8.6: 18.6%

If a system has a TAN of 2.0 mg/L at pH 8.0 and 25°C, the toxic $NH_3$ is $2.0 \times 0.0541 = 0.1082 \text{ mg/L}$ (which falls in the chronic stress / acute danger zone).

## Specific Parameter Ranges for Tropical Aquariums
For a standard tropical freshwater community aquarium, specific ranges are critical for homeostasis.

- **pH:** Ideal range is **6.5 – 7.5**. (Discus: 6.0-7.0; African Cichlids: 7.8-8.5).
- **General Hardness (GH):** **4 – 12 °d (approx. 70 – 200 mg/L)**. This measures calcium (target 30-80 mg/L) and magnesium (target 5-15 mg/L) ions.
- **Carbonate Hardness (KH):** **4 – 8 °d (approx. 70 – 140 mg/L)**. Acts as a buffer to stabilize pH. Levels below 3 °d risk pH crashes.
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO):** Ideal range is **5 – 8 mg/L**. Levels below 3 mg/L are stressful/fatal.
- **Nitrogenous Waste:** Ammonia $NH_3$ (0 mg/L), Nitrite $NO_2$ (0 mg/L), Nitrate $NO_3$ (< 20 mg/L).
