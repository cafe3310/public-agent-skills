# Product: Deep Research Visualizer

A real-time analytical dashboard for autonomous research agents.

## Users
- Data analysts and researchers performing deep-dive investigations.
- Software engineers monitoring agent task execution.
- Decision makers reviewing data-rich synthesis reports.

## Register
product

## Brand & Tone
- **Aesthetic**: Technical, industrial, high-density. "Zed-like" hard-edged design.
- **Tone**: Austere, precise, professional.
- **Keywords**: Robust, Performance, Clarity, Depth.

## Anti-references
- Soft, rounded "SaaS" aesthetics.
- Excessive whitespace.
- Playful or conversational UI elements.
- Heavy shadows and glassmorphism.

## Strategic Principles
- **Data First**: Content is the priority. UI frames the data with minimal interference.
- **Panel Density**: Efficient use of space through clear panel boundaries.
- **Precision**: 1px sharp borders and consistent alignment.
- **Contrast**: High functional contrast for readability in technical environments.
